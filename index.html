Executive Biography & Research Foundation

// ORIGIN My research path originates at the intersection of competitive high-stakes telemetry systems and doctrinal legal analysis, which evolved into a dedicated pursuit of deep tech law, post-quantum cryptographic migration governance, and systems-level vulnerability analysis. My core methodological approach combines ex-ante regulatory impact assessments (RIA) with empirical, functional comparative analysis across the EU, US, and PRC legal architectures.

I am an Information Security Governance & Tech Law Researcher based in Warsaw, currently serving as Vice President of the Management Board and Co-Founder at the "SAVE THE CLICK" Foundation. Operating at the doctrinal interface between computer science and law, my work focuses on defining regulatory baselines across emerging frontiers, including post-quantum cryptography, AI civil liability (Revised PLD & AI Act), and distributed processing systems.

My academic path is built on an Individually Accelerated Degree Pathway at the War Studies University (Akademia Sztuki Wojennej), concurrently compressing the final three years (Years 3, 4, and 5) of the 5-year uniform Master of Laws syllabus into a single academic year while maintaining a top academic record (Cumulative GPA: 4.9+/5.0).

Core Research Agenda & Technical Foundation

Rather than analyzing retroactive case law, my research focus is predictive, empirical, and stress-test-driven, concentrating on four primary pillars:

  • Comparative Architectures of Cyber-Governance & Sovereign Tech: Conducting comparative, multijurisdictional analyses of primary statutory sources, national security mandates, and regulatory baselines across leading global technological blocks (EU NIS2/CRA, US Presidential EOs/NIST Standards, PRC TC260/CSL/DSL/MLPS 2.0). Stress-testing sovereign frameworks against post-quantum cryptographic disruption.
  • Liability Allocation in Decentralized & AI Architectures: Analyzing regulatory friction and the imputation of strict product liability under the Revised Product Liability Directive (PLD) and the EU AI Act concerning distributed machine learning architectures (Federated Learning).
  • Gaming Ecosystem Governance, Anti-Cheat Protocols & Client-Side Telemetry: Investigating statutory boundaries of ring-0 (kernel-level) anti-cheat drivers, continuous memory telemetry, and platform terms-of-service enforcement under GDPR, DSA, and the Revised PLD. DOMAIN EXPERTISE: 5+ years Tier-1 competitive e-sports background (Grandmaster ranking, EU Top 10), providing practitioner-level forensic familiarity with proprietary game engine anti-tamper mechanisms, memory manipulation exploits, and kernel-space client integrity systems.
  • Technical Systems Analysis & Standards Engineering: Applied post-quantum cryptographic migration standards (NIST SP 800-series, FIPS 203/204/205), ISO/IEC 27001, PRC Standardization frameworks (TC260 GB/T, GM/T, MLPS 2.0). Kernel driver models (Windows/Linux), LaTeX typesetting, and POSIX/Git development environments.
